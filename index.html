One step first

Your board emails updates to your whole team. District email often sends these to Junk, so add the board's address to your safe senders before you start.

  1. Open Outlook on the web at outlook.office.com and sign in with your school account.
  2. Select the gear icon, then Mail, then Junk email.
  3. Under Safe senders and domains, select Add, paste the address, and press Enter.
  4. Select Save.

Using the Outlook app on a phone? Open any board email, select the three dots, and choose Add to safe senders.
